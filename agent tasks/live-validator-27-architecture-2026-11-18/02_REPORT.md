## Eligibility and refusal

A future narrow evaluation needs one saved original row, the pinned target and trace/attachment ownership and a bounded occurrence interval **E = [q − u, q + u]**. For a pre-enrollment positive, require all of **E** inside one independently supported interval **[L, U)**. **L** is no earlier than both the first verified fresh eligible sample's completion and the supported availability bound for the sensor. **U** is the earliest of the actual access origin, the end of supported continuity, a possible neighboring run transition, or target lifetime end. For an uncertain lower boundary take its latest plausible end, and for an uncertain upper boundary take its earliest plausible start. The last non-run read and first eligible read bound a transition. They do not uniquely label the events between them. A positive delivery establishes provider activity for that event alone. No cited row meets the full conditions, and the existing v3 wire cannot encode pre-origin offsets by clipping them to zero.

Binding must refuse in these cases.

- The event could refer to a recycled target PID, crosses attach/exit, or can belong to another attachment or trace. One PID or executable path does not establish the game object. Discovery of two matching processes prevents initial attach, but a second same-image process appearing later can still defeat a path-only attribution. For source PID reuse and foreign handle reuse, require independent lifetime evidence. A source lifetime and sampled granted right do not make a foreign 4663 handle lifetime continuous.
- Sample data are cached, stale, failed, loading, ambiguous, or a physical fresh-sample gap exceeds 500 ms. Fresh and compatible endpoints still do not identify activity inside an unobserved gap. A paused candidate can live beyond its active-time limit. Do not use that pause to assert continuous coverage. No seed, player pointer, stale playing or game-over flag, one menuMusic probe, diagnostic-only read or Scanner OFF/SEARCHING/FOUND can replace the trusted boundary. Current stable-menu checks depend on repeated fresh evidence and timer continuity (`engine.py:498–527,1640–1649`).
- Old work is finalizing while a new attempt is starting, or the row uncertainty intersects the two owners. Filter by occurrence and the frozen ownership, with no reuse of ambient `localRunId` or delivery order. `engine.py:875–882,1027–1121,1144–1162` freezes the old owner, creates a fresh monitor and defers new audit setup. Unavailable handoff time remains unknown.
- ETW is unready or lost data, evidence was dropped or target checks failed, or the required clock brackets are absent, too wide, from different boots or inconsistent across a Windows clock change. A recorded positive may survive an unrelated loss. Loss survives alongside it. A UTC step does not reorder QPC events within a boot, but it can invalidate source-creation or cross-sensor joins.

Read-only test expectations consulted include Validator `tests/test_run_detection.py:95–195` for bursts, pauses, short attempts, gaps and menu, `tests/test_validator_engine.py:164–222` for attachment and marker, `tests/test_process_access.py:171–204,592–650` for unassigned and startup Census, and `tests/test_validator_rights_correlation.py:39–108,162–173` for source and target identities, reuse, clock domains and capture watermark. Current source caps each callback and binding queue at **2,048** rows with at most **1,536** routine entries. One resolving metadata batch can hold up to a binding queue's worth. Run evidence has **256** pending rows and **64** groups, and local diagnostics have **24** groups (`direct_open.py:18–58`, `process_access.py:13–17,65–74,430–458`, `direct_open_evidence.py:9–12,75–96,124–149`). ETW health checks are about 1 s apart, with more frequent drains. Native trace configuration requests 64 KiB × 16–64 buffers and a 1 s flush timer. Neither specifies a peak arrival rate or delivery bound. The observed first-drain delays of 1.013 s in C01 and 2.266 s in IPC are isolated measurements.

A comparison-only cap of **256 packed slots × 64 bytes = 16,384 bytes** uses the existing pending-row count. The layout can hold four 64-bit time fields (occurrence, uncertainty, arrival and UTC), a 64-bit sequence, four 32-bit fields (source PID, mask, status and trace generation) and eight bytes for flags or an identity reference. With a 64-byte header for one pinned target and attachment, that is **16,448 bytes** per active packed ring. If retained by 12 pending finalizers plus the active monitor, the packed storage would reach **213,824 bytes**. A ring would be cleared at handoff, with no storage passed to the next run. The calculation covers packed storage only. Python heap and CPU cost were not measured. Additional source identity tables, handles, names or objects would need separate accounting.
